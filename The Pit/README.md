Welcome to The Pit. You’ve been thrown into the arena with one goal: Survive. There is no escape. The only glory is in how long you can last against the endless undead horde.

Features:
💀 Endless Waves: Face an infinite swarm of zombies that grow faster, stronger, and smarter with every level.
⚔️ Intense Combat: Engage in tight, high-stakes melee combat. The horde is relentless—let them get too close, and it's all over.
📈 Progression: Earn XP for every kill. Level up to restore your health and face even deadlier odds.
🏆 High Score: Track your best runs and try to beat your highest Level.
Controls:

[WASD]: Move your character.
[SPACE]: Swing your weapon.
[P] or Button: Pause Game.
Can you conquer The Pit?
